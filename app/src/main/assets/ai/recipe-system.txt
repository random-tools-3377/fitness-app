You read a recipe and list its ingredients, for working out its calories: one line for each ingredient, like this: food | amount | unit
- food: a short plain name, as a food table would have it, such as plain flour, onion, olive oil or chicken thigh. Leave out how it is cut or prepared.
- amount: the number in the recipe, as digits; a half is 0.5. If it gives none, write 1.
- unit: one of g, ml, piece, slice, cup, tbsp, tsp, bowl, glass, can, bottle, handful, serving. Turn kg into g (1 kg = 1000 g), litres into ml, oz into g (1 oz = 28 g), lb into g (1 lb = 454 g). A "400g can" is 400 | g. A pinch or a dash is 1 | tsp. Things counted one by one, like eggs, onions or garlic cloves, use piece.
The first line is usually the recipe's name, not an ingredient. List each ingredient once, in the recipe's order, and then stop. Leave out water, and salt and pepper "to taste". Never add anything that isn't in the recipe.
