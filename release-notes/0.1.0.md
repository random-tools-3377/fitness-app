The first version to try: the food diary.

**What you can do**

- Log what you eat in a **diary** split into breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Each food, meal and day shows calories and protein, carbs and fat. The arrows at the top go to other days.
- **Find foods** in the built-in list of about 13,400 common foods and dishes, from the US government's food data (USDA). Foods you have eaten lately and often come first.
- **Type in a food from its label**: per 100 g, per 100 ml or per serving, in kcal or kJ. The app remembers it, with its own portions like "1 slice".
- Log amounts in **grams or millilitres**, or in a food's own **portions** ("1 cup", "1 large").
- **Quick add**: just a number of calories, with no food.
- **Save a meal** (the ⋮ menu on a meal) and add it again in one go. **Copy** a meal or a whole day from another day.
- Tap anything in the diary to change its amount, move it to another meal, or remove it.
- Work out your **daily calorie target** from your age, height, weight, sex and how active you are, and whether you want to lose, keep or gain weight (Settings, or the button on the diary). You can always type your own number instead.
- **Protein, carbs and fat targets**: 25% / 50% / 25% of the calories to start, changeable in percentages or grams.
- **Water**: a 2-litre daily target, with buttons for a glass and a bottle. All three sizes can be changed in Settings.
- **Weigh-ins** with a chart and a 7-day average line. When a new weigh-in moves the worked-out target by 50 kcal or more, the app offers the new number. It never changes your target by itself.
- **Units** follow the phone's region, and can be switched in Settings: kg or lb, cm or ft/in, kcal or kJ, ml or fl oz.
- **Export** everything to a file, and **import** it back (Settings, Your data).

**Good to know**

- Carbs are counted without fibre, as on EU food labels, for every food. When typing in a US label, take the fibre off the total carbohydrate.
- The calorie target is an estimate to start from, not medical advice. The app never suggests less than 1,200 kcal a day for women or 1,500 for men, and says why.

**Not in this version yet**

- European foods from France's food table (CIQUAL). Its website isn't answering at the moment; the app will take them in as soon as it does, in a small update.
- Workouts and calories burned (0.2.0), barcode scanning (0.3.0), streaks, more charts, reminders, the privacy lock and automatic backups (0.4.0).

**Private and offline**

The app has no internet access and is left out of phone backups. Everything stays on the phone, so export now and then and keep the file safe: uninstalling the app deletes your diary.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.1.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and allow installing from your browser if Android asks. Later versions install over this one and keep your data.
