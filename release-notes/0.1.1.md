A small update: bring in your own food list from a spreadsheet.

**What's new**

- **Import foods from a spreadsheet.** On the Foods tab, "Save a template" saves a file to fill in, in Google Sheets, Excel, Numbers or any spreadsheet app: one food per row, with its name, brand, calories (kcal or kJ), protein, carbs and fat, per 100 g, per 100 ml or per serving, and its own portions like `1 slice=30`. Save it as CSV, then press "Import a spreadsheet" and pick the file.
- Before anything is added, the app shows how many foods it found, and which rows it left out and why (no calories, numbers that can't be right, or a food you already have). Nothing you already have is changed.
- Spreadsheets saved the European way, with semicolons and decimal commas (`12,5`), are read too.

Everything from 0.1.0 is still here: the food diary, the built-in foods, the calorie target, water, weight, and export and import of all your data.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.1.1.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your data.
