A home-screen widget, steps from the phone, light or dark and a colour, and saving your data as spreadsheets.

**What's new**

- **Home-screen widget.** Long-press the home screen, choose Widgets, and find Fitness App. It shows today's calories left and water, with **+ Glass** to add a glass of water and **Add food** to open the app at the right meal. If the diary is behind the privacy lock, the widget shows no numbers.
- **Steps** (Settings → Steps). Turn it on to read your step count from Health Connect, the phone's own store of health data, which works offline. Android 14 and later have it built in; on older phones, install the Health Connect app first. The diary shows the day's steps, and they add to the calories you can eat: about 35 kcal per 1,000 steps at 70 kg. Steps during a workout you logged aren't counted twice, and the "count only half" setting applies to them too. Only the step count is read.
- **Look** (Settings → Look). Light, dark, or follow the phone, and a colour: the phone's own colours (Android 12 and later), terracotta, green, blue or purple.
- **Save as spreadsheets** (Settings → Your data). Saves your diary, weigh-ins, measurements, workouts, and sleep and mood as spreadsheet (CSV) files in one zip file, to open in a spreadsheet app. This is for reading; to move your data to another phone, use Export as before.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.10.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data. The first time you turn on steps, the phone asks to allow it.
