Alcohol and caffeine, vitamins and minerals, targets that change by day, and diet breaks.

**What's new**

- **Alcohol and caffeine.** The day shows the alcohol you've had as standard drinks, and your caffeine in mg, from built-in foods and drinks and from ones you type in (there are two new boxes on the label form). Set daily limits in Settings, under **Fibre, sugar, saturates, salt, alcohol and caffeine**. A standard drink is 10 g of alcohol to start (14 g if your phone is set to the US, 8 g, a "unit", in the UK); you can change it.
- **Vitamins and minerals.** When you log built-in foods, the diary shows iron, calcium, vitamin D, vitamin C, potassium, magnesium, vitamin B12, folate, zinc and vitamin A, as a share of the reference values printed on EU labels. These are a guide, not your own needs. Foods you type in and country files don't have them.
- **Targets by day** (Settings → Targets by day). Give any weekday its own calorie target, and add extra calories on training days: days with a finished workout, or a workout in your weekly programme. The diary says when a day's target differs, and the stats and streaks use each day's own target.
- **Diet breaks** (also under Targets by day). Spend one or two weeks at the calories that keep your weight the same, then go back to your plan by itself.

**Not in this version yet**

- The body-fat estimate, supplements and medication, and cycle tracking (0.12.0).
