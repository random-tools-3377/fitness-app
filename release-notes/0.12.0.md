A body-fat estimate, supplements and medication, and cycle tracking.

**What's new**

- **Body-fat estimate** (Progress → Body). Once you've measured your waist and neck (and hips, for women) and filled in your height under the daily target, the app estimates your body fat with the US Navy formula. It's an estimate and can be a few points off; the trend over time is what's useful.
- **Supplements and medication** (Progress → Habits). Add what you take, with a reminder time if you like, and tick it off each day in the diary. On the lock screen, reminders say only "A reminder from Fitness App", not what you take. The app gives no advice about them.
- **Cycle tracking.** Off to start: turn it on in Settings → Privacy. Then log your period under Progress → Body with **Period started today** and **Last day today**. The app shows the day of your cycle, your average cycle and the next period as an estimate, and the weight chart shades period days, because weight often goes up a little around then. It's kept on this phone, behind the lock if Progress is locked, and it's not for planning or avoiding pregnancy.
- Exports and the daily backup now include periods. A file from this version can't be opened by an older one.

**Not in this version yet**

- The interval timer, the one-rep-max table and exercise notes (0.13.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.12.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
