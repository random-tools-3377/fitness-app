An interval timer, the weights for every number of reps, and notes on exercises.

**What's new**

- **Interval timer** (Workouts → Interval timer). Rounds of work and rest, with a buzz at each change: Tabata (20 seconds on, 10 off, 8 rounds), HIIT (40 on, 20 off, 10 rounds), or your own. The screen stays on while it runs. To count the calories, log it afterwards with **Log cardio**, as "Circuit or HIIT class".
- **Weights for 1 to 12 reps** (Workouts → Personal bests). For each exercise with an estimated one-rep max, tap **Weights for 1 to 12 reps** to see what you could lift for each number of reps. These are estimates from your best.
- **Exercise notes.** In a workout, tap **Add a note** under an exercise's name to keep your own cue, like "elbows in". It shows every time you do that exercise.

**Not in this version yet**

- The calendar, year in review, larger text and colour-blind-friendly charts (0.14.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.13.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
