A calendar, a year in review, larger text and colour-blind-friendly charts.

**What's new**

- **Calendar** (Progress → Calendar). A month at a glance: each day is coloured by how it went against that day's calorie target (within 10%, under or over), and days with a workout have a dot. Swipe through months with the arrows.
- **Year in review** (Progress → Year). This year so far, and earlier years: days logged, your average, days on target, workouts, the total you lifted, cardio, personal bests set, how your weight changed, and where your calories came from.
- **Larger text** (Settings → Look). Make the app's text larger than the phone's own setting, or largest.
- **Colour-blind-friendly charts** (Settings → Look). Charts and the calendar use blue and orange, which are easy to tell apart with any kind of colour blindness.

**This completes the second list**

With this version, everything planned so far is in.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.14.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
