**What's new**

- **Features** (Settings → Features, at the top). Each part of the app has a switch. When a part is off, its tab, buttons, diary cards and settings all disappear; nothing you logged is deleted, and switching it back on brings it all back.
- **A simple start.** A new install shows only the food diary, saved meals, water, weight, workouts, and stats and streaks. Everything else is listed under **You might like**, with a line on what it does, to switch on when you want it.
- **Updating keeps what you use.** If you already use a part (it has something logged, or a setting changed), it stays on. Other extras are now off, but they're one switch away on the Features page.
- Cycle tracking is switched on and off on the Features page now, instead of under Privacy.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.15.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
