Better label reading, the first optional on-phone AI part.

**What's new**

- **Better label reading** (Settings → Features → AI on this phone). A larger reading model reads more of a nutrition label's numbers right, especially on photos taken at a slight angle. It's optional and off to start: the app works fully without it, and reads labels the usual way until you add it.
- **How to add it.** Switch it on, then on the phone open this app's Releases page, find **AI models (optional)**, and download `fitness-ai-labels.zip` (27 MB). Back in the app, tap **Open the model file** and pick it. It stays on the phone and needs no internet. You can remove it again on the same page.
- As before, you check every number before the food is saved.

**Coming next**

- Describing a meal in words, pasting a recipe, and a weekly summary in plain words (0.17.0); voice (0.18.0); suggestions from a photo of a plate (0.19.0). All optional, all on the phone.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.16.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data. The app is bigger than before, for the part that runs the model (it's there even if you never add the model).
