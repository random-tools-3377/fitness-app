More ways to change how the app looks, like in the checklist app. All in Settings → Look.

**What's new**

- **Pure black**, as well as light, dark and following the phone. It saves power on many screens.
- **Colours.** Ready-made ones (now with Ocean, Forest and Sunset), your wallpaper's colours, or **any colour** from a colour picker: a colour square, a hue slider and a hex code box, with a preview. The whole app is built from the colour you pick, and text stays readable.
- **Card size** (compact, comfortable or large), and a **smaller** text size as well as larger.
- **Fonts**: the phone's own, rounded (Nunito), easy to read (Atkinson Hyperlegible) or serif (Lora).
- **Behind the diary**: nothing, a gradient of the app's colour, or your own photo, dimmed so the text stays readable. The photo stays on the phone and isn't in the backup file.
- **Tabs**: hide the ones you don't use (a hidden tab can still be opened from Settings), and choose the tab the app opens on.
- **App icon**: terracotta, ocean, forest or night.
- **Widget**: solid in the app's colours, or see-through over your wallpaper.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.17.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data. Because the app can now change its icon, some home screens drop the old icon from the home screen once: if it's gone, add it again from the app list.
