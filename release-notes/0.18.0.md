More optional AI, all on the phone: describe a meal in words or out loud, find the foods on a photo of your plate, paste a recipe, and read your week in a few plain sentences.

**What's new**

Each of these is off to start. Switch them on in **Settings → Features → AI on this phone**. The app works fully without any of them.

- **Describe a meal.** On the screen for adding food, tap **Describe it** and type what you had, like "two eggs and a slice of toast with butter". The AI picks out each food and amount and finds it among your foods and the built-in ones. You see the list first: tap a food to pick another, change any amount, untick what's wrong, then add them.
- **Say a meal.** The same, out loud: tap **Say it**, speak, and tap **Stop**. What you say is turned into text on the phone and then forgotten; the recording is never saved. The first time, the phone asks to let the app use the microphone.
- **Photo of a plate.** Take or choose a photo of your meal, and the AI suggests the foods it can see, one serving each for you to change.
- **Paste a recipe.** In Plan → Recipes, tap **Paste a recipe** and paste the recipe's text. The AI lists the ingredients and amounts, the app reads the name, servings and method, and you check it all in the recipe screen before saving.
- **The week in words.** In Progress → Stats, on the week view, tap **Write it** for a few plain sentences about your week, written from the numbers on that page.

The AI only picks out words; every number comes from the app's own food data, and nothing is added or saved until you say so. It never gives medical advice.

**Adding the AI files**

They are big, one-time downloads, on this app's Releases page under **AI models (optional)**:

- `fitness-ai-text.zip` (1.3 GB): for describing a meal, pasting a recipe and the week in words.
- `fitness-ai-speech.zip` (1 GB): for saying a meal, as well as the one above.
- `fitness-ai-plate.zip` (0.5 GB): for photos of a plate.

Download the ones you want on the phone, then in **Settings → Features → AI on this phone** tap **Open an AI file** and pick it. Opening a big file takes a few minutes and needs about twice its size free; you can delete the download afterwards.

**Good to know**

- These AI parts need a 64-bit phone (almost every phone from the last few years). On a phone with less than about 4 GB of memory they may be slow or not start.
- They take a few seconds to a minute, depending on the phone.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.18.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
