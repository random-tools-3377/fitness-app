Change a built-in food once, and use your own version from then on.

**What's new**

- **Make my own copy.** When a built-in food isn't quite right (your local bread, say, has different numbers), tap it as if adding it, then tap **Not quite right? Make my own copy**. The food form opens filled in from the built-in food. Change what you need, like the name, calories or portions, and save.
- From then on your copy takes the original's place: in the food search, in recent foods and in **Describe a meal**. The original is hidden, not deleted.
- If you delete your copy, the original comes back.
- Days already logged keep the numbers they had.
