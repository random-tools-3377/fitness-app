Workouts are here: weights, cardio, bodyweight and timed exercises, with a rest timer and personal bests.

**What's new**

- A **Workouts** tab. Start a workout, add exercises from the built-in list of about 55 common ones (or add your own), and tick each set as you do it.
  - **Weights**: the weight and the reps for each set.
  - **Bodyweight**, like push-ups: the reps.
  - **Timed**, like a plank: the seconds.
  - **Cardio**, like a run: the time and, if you know it, the distance. "Log cardio" types in a run, walk or ride on its own, with no GPS.
- **Saved workouts.** When you finish, give the workout a name, like "Leg day". Next time, tap it and every exercise fills in with the weights and reps from last time.
- **Rest timer.** Ticking a set starts a 90-second rest. Tap "Rest 1:30" on an exercise to change its rest. When the rest is over, the phone buzzes, and if the app is in the background, a notification says so. The app asks once to allow notifications for this.
- A workout in progress is kept as you go, so closing the app loses nothing. Come back with "Continue".
- **Personal bests**: the heaviest weight, the most reps at a weight, an estimated one-rep max, and the longest and fastest cardio. Beating one is celebrated on the spot. All your bests are under "Personal bests" on the Workouts tab.
- **Calories burned** are added to the day's allowance in the diary. They are estimated from standard activity values, times your latest weigh-in and the time spent. For cardio with a distance, your speed counts too. Estimates tend to run high, so Settings can count only half.

**Good to know**

- Calories burned need your weight: add a weigh-in first.
- Exports now include workouts. Files exported from 0.1 still import.

**Not in this version yet**

- Barcode scanning (0.3.0), streaks, more stats and charts, reminders, the privacy lock and automatic backups (0.4.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.2.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
