Workouts that fit the way you train: log what you like for each exercise, plan workouts ahead, and set rests and circuits your way.

**What's new**

- **Log what you like.** Weights, Bodyweight, Timed and Cardio are now just tags. For each exercise, choose what to log: weight, reps, time in seconds or minutes, distance, or any mix. For example, count jumping jacks in reps. In a workout, tap an exercise's **⋮** and then **What to log…**. The choice is kept for that exercise. New exercises of your own get a tag and your choice too.
- **More exercises built in:** jumping jacks, mountain climbers, high knees, butt kicks, skater and tuck jumps, bear crawl, push-up variants (knee, incline, decline, wide, diamond, pike, archer, wall), jump squats and lunges, box jumps, step-ups, inverted rows, bench dips, bicycle crunches, Russian twists, V-ups, dead bugs, bird dogs, hollow holds, L-sits, goblet squats, Bulgarian split squats and more.
- **Plan a workout ahead.** On the Workouts tab, tap **Plan a workout ahead** to set up a workout's exercises, sets, numbers and rests before you start it. To change a saved workout later, tap its pencil.
- **Circuits for everyone.** Tap an exercise's **⋮** and then **Do in a circuit with the next one**. You do one set of each exercise in turn, like 20 push-ups, 10 squats and 20 leg raises, and rest only after the round. A circuit can have as many exercises as you like, and you can move exercises up and down.
- **A rest for each set.** Tap a set's number and choose **Rest after this set**. Other sets keep the exercise's rest.
- **Keep the screen on** while a workout is open: in the workout's **⋮** menu, or in **Settings → Workouts**. It is off to start.
- **Started by mistake?** If you leave a workout within its first minute with nothing ticked, it isn't kept.
- **Calories from reps.** Sets counted in reps now count for the work they are: about 3 seconds a rep (1 to 2 seconds for quick moves like jumping jacks), at the exercise's effort. If the workout's length gives more, that counts instead, as before. For example, 120 push-ups ticked off in 15 seconds now count about 30 kcal for a 75 kg person, not 1.

Workouts you've already done keep their calories.
