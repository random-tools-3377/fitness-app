Quicker workouts: change all of an exercise's sets at once, reps till failure, and whole circuits in one tap.

**What's new**

- **All sets at once.** Each exercise has a row with **−** and **+** for the number of sets, the reps, the weight and the time. A change applies to every set you haven't ticked yet. Tap the number to type it instead. When all the sets are the same, they show as small numbered buttons to tick. Tap **Each set** to see and change them one by one.
- **Reps till failure.** Turn on **Till failure** next to the reps, or tap a set's number and choose **Reps till failure**. No number is planned. When you tick the set, the app asks how many you managed, and you can leave it empty.
- **Moving inside a circuit.** **Move up** and **Move down** now work for exercises in a circuit: they swap places inside it. At the circuit's edge, moving further takes the exercise out. To move the whole circuit, use **Move the whole circuit up/down**.
- **All one circuit.** In the workout's **⋮** menu, or at the top of a planned workout, one tap puts every exercise in one circuit. **No circuits** undoes it.
- **Round done.** Under a circuit, tap **Round done** to tick one set of every exercise in it at once. The rest timer then starts once.
