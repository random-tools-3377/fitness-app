Finding foods by type, the European foods first if you like, and a protein target that follows your weight and how active you are.

**What's new**

- **Food types.** Under the search box, tap a type to see only those foods: meals and dishes, meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans and lentils, nuts, vegetables, fruit, grains, bread, breakfast cereals, oils, sweets, snacks, drinks, or sauces and spices. Type to narrow it down further, or tap **All** to go back. The types come from the food tables' own groups, so they're only on the built-in foods, not yours. The tables don't say how processed a food is, so there's no "processed" type.
- **European or US foods first.** In **Settings → Food search**, choose which built-in foods come first when both have a match. To start with, it follows your phone's region: European in Europe, US in the US. Your own foods, favourites and foods you've used still come first.
- **Protein by body weight.** The protein target now comes from your weight and how active you are, not a quarter of your calories:
  - mostly sitting: 0.9 g per kg
  - lightly active: 1.4 g per kg
  - moderately active: 1.6 g per kg
  - active: 1.8 g per kg
  - very active: 2.0 g per kg

  Fat is 25% of the calories, and carbs are the rest. You can type your own grams per kg, or go back to percentages or grams, in **Settings → Protein, carbs and fat**. If you had changed your targets before, they stay as you set them.
