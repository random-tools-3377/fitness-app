Barcode scanning, with no internet: scan a packaged food to add it.

**What's new**

- A **Scan** button when adding a food. Point the camera at the barcode and the product comes up with its calories, protein, carbs and fat, ready to add. The app asks to use the camera the first time. Scanning happens on the phone; nothing goes online.
- **Country files.** The products come from a file for your country, which you add once:
  1. On the phone, open the app's Releases page and find **Country files for barcode scanning**. There is a file for 85 countries, every EU country included. Tap yours to download it.
  2. In the app, go to **Foods**, press **Add a country file**, and pick the downloaded file. A big country takes a minute or so.
  3. Add as many countries as you like, and remove them on the same screen. Download them again now and then for newer products.
- **A barcode in no file?** The app offers to type it in from the label, once. After that, scanning it finds your food.
- Products you have added before show among your recent and frequent foods.

**Good to know**

- The product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free database anyone can add to, so now and then a product is missing or has a mistake. The United States file also has the US government's branded-food data.
- Carbs are given without fibre, as on EU labels, for every product, the US ones included.

**Not in this version yet**

- Streaks, more stats and charts, reminders, the privacy lock and automatic backups (0.4.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.3.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
