Streaks, stats and charts, reminders, a privacy lock and automatic backups.

**What's new**

- A **Progress** tab, in place of Weight, with two parts: **Stats and streaks**, and **Weight** (weigh-ins and the chart, as before).
- **Streaks**: days with food logged; days on target (within 10% of your calorie target, either side); weeks with your number of workouts, so rest days don't break it; and days you reached your water target. Each shows how long it is now and your best. Today doesn't break a streak until it's over.
- **Stats** for the last week, month or year:
  - calories each day against your target, with the daily average and the days on target
  - the average protein, carbs and fat
  - the foods that gave the most calories
  - your weight trend and, if you set a goal weight, roughly when you'd reach it at this pace (an estimate)
  - workouts a week, the total lifted (sets × reps × weight) by week, by exercise and by muscle group, the calories burned, and water

  The charts are drawn by the app itself.
- **Reminders** (Settings), all off until you turn them on: one for each meal, water every few hours during the day, workouts and weigh-ins on the days you choose. They're set on the phone itself and come back after a restart. Tapping one opens the right screen.
- **Privacy lock** (Settings, Privacy): choose which tabs open only with your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN. They lock again each time you leave the app. The app is also hidden in the recent-apps screen.
- **Automatic backup** (Settings): once a day, when you open the app, everything is saved to a folder you choose, keeping the newest 7. A folder on a memory card, or one a cloud app keeps in step, keeps a copy off the phone.
- **Goal weight** (optional) on the calorie target screen, for the stats.
- **Workouts a week** (Settings, Workouts), for the weekly streak: 3 to start.

**Not in this version yet**

- Recipes, planning meals ahead and the shopping list (0.5.0), a target for each meal and the fasting timer (0.6.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.4.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
