Recipes, planning meals ahead, and a shopping list.

**What's new**

- A **Plan** tab, with Meals, Shopping list and Recipes.
- **Recipes.** Add the foods that go in, and how many servings it makes; the app works out the calories and protein, carbs and fat in each serving. If you weigh the finished dish once, you can log it by weight, which allows for water lost or gained in cooking. There's room for the method too. Recipes are found like any food, to log or to plan. Changing a recipe applies from then on; days already logged keep what they were.
- **Planning meals ahead.** Put foods, saved meals and recipes on the coming days. Each day shows its planned calories and protein, carbs and fat against your target, so you can balance it before you eat it. On the day, planned items show in the diary; tick **Eaten** to log one. Planned items you don't tick don't count. Copy a day's plan to the next day, or a week's plan to the next week.
- **Shopping list.** Pick the days, and the app makes a list from what's planned, adding up the same food across the days and breaking recipes into their foods. Tick things off in the shop, and add your own items, like washing-up liquid. Making a new list clears what you ticked.
- **Settings** has moved to the gear button at the top of the diary, to make room for the Plan tab.

**Not in this version yet**

- A target for each meal and the fasting timer (0.6.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.5.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
