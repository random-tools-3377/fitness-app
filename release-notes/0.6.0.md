A target for each meal, and a fasting timer.

**What's new**

- **A target for each meal** (Settings, under protein, carbs and fat). It's off until you turn it on. Your day's target is split between breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks: 25%, 35%, 30% and 10% to start, changeable in percentages or calories. Protein, carbs and fat follow the same split. Each meal in the diary then shows what's left of its own share. Going over in one meal doesn't take from the others; the day's total still counts.
- **Fasting timer** (Plan tab, Fasting). Choose 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 or 23:1, or your own hours, and start. The ring shows how long you've fasted and how long is left, and a notification says when the fast is done. If you log food during a fast, the app asks whether to end it. Past fasts are kept, and each planned fast you finish counts toward a streak on the Progress tab.
- Exports now include fasts.
- **European foods.** The built-in list now also has about 2,300 foods and dishes from France's food table (CIQUAL), with English names, alongside the US list. Search for them as usual in Add food.

The app gives no medical advice about fasting.

**This completes the plan**

With this version, everything in the original design is in: the food diary, workouts, barcodes, streaks and stats, reminders, the privacy lock, backups, recipes, meal plans, the shopping list, meal targets and fasting.

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.6.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
