More about what's in your food, favourites, and reading a label with the camera.

**What's new**

- **Fibre, sugar, saturates and salt.** Foods now show these four as well, where the food's data has them, and the day adds them up under protein, carbs and fat. You can type them in from a label too. In Settings, under **Fibre, sugar, saturates and salt**, you can set a daily target for each: fibre as "at least", the others as "at most". Leave a box empty for no target. Foods you logged before keep what they had.
- **Read a label with the camera.** When typing in a food, tap **Take a photo** (or **Choose a photo**) of the nutrition table. The phone reads the numbers itself, with nothing sent anywhere, and fills in the form. It says how many it found. **Always check them against the label before saving**: a blurry or curved label can be misread. A US label's carbs include fibre, so the fibre is taken off, as everywhere else in the app.
- **Favourites.** Tap a food, then **Make it a favourite**. Favourites have a ★ and come first, both in the list before you type and in the search.
- **Same as yesterday.** An empty meal has a **Same as yesterday** button that copies what you had at that meal the day before. It's also in each meal's ⋮ menu.
- Spreadsheet imports can have columns for fibre, sugar, saturated fat and salt (or sodium in mg). The template has them.

**Not in this version yet**

- Body measurements, progress photos, sleep and mood, the weekly check-in and your own habits (0.8.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.7.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data. The first time you take a photo of a label, the phone asks to allow the camera.
