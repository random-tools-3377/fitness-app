Body measurements, progress photos, sleep and mood, your own habits, and a weekly check-in.

**What's new**

- **Progress** now has four parts: Stats, Weight, Body and Habits.
- **Body measurements** (Progress → Body). Waist, hips, chest, arm, thigh, neck and body fat %. Fill in any you like, whenever you like. Each shows its latest value and how much it has changed since the first. Lengths are in cm, or inches if you use ft/in for height.
- **Progress photos** (Progress → Body). Take one or choose one from the gallery. They're kept only inside the app on this phone: not in the gallery, not in exports or backups. Tap a photo to see it bigger, save a copy somewhere you choose, or delete it. To keep them behind your phone's lock, tick Progress in Settings → Privacy.
- **Sleep and mood.** At the bottom of the diary, note how many hours you slept and pick a mood from Low to Great. Progress → Habits shows the last 14 days next to the calories you ate, to spot patterns.
- **Your own habits** (Progress → Habits). Add things like "No sugary drinks" or "Stretch 10 minutes", and tick them in the diary each day. Each gets a streak like the built-in ones. Stopping a habit keeps its history.
- **Weekly check-in.** On Mondays, if you weighed in at least twice in each of the last two weeks, the Progress tab shows how your average weight moved against your plan. If your target looks off by 50 kcal or more, it offers a new one; it never changes it by itself. It's an estimate, not medical advice.
- Exports and the daily backup now include measurements, sleep and mood, and habits (not photos). A file from this version can't be opened by an older one.

**Not in this version yet**

- Workout programmes, the plate calculator, "try a bit more" hints, supersets and warm-up sets (0.9.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.8.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
