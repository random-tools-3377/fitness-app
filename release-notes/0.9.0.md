A weekly programme, a plate calculator, "try a bit more" hints, supersets and warm-up sets.

**What's new**

- **Weekly programme** (Workouts tab). Pick which saved workout to do on which days, like Push on Monday, Pull on Wednesday and Legs on Friday. On those days the Workouts tab has a button to start today's session; other days are rest days.
- **"Try a bit more."** When you did every set of an exercise last time, the next session suggests a little more: 2.5 kg (or 5 lb) more for weights, or one more rep for bodyweight exercises. Tap **Use it** to fill it in, or ignore it.
- **Plate calculator.** In an exercise's ⋮ menu: type the weight you want on the bar, and it shows which plates go on each side. Set your bar and plates in Settings → Workouts (20 kg bar and 25–1.25 kg plates to start, or 45 lb and 45–2.5 lb).
- **Supersets.** In an exercise's ⋮ menu, **Superset with the next exercise** joins them. Do one set of each in turn; the rest timer starts only after the last one of the round. Saved workouts keep their supersets.
- **Warm-up sets.** Tap a set's number to mark it as a warm-up (it shows **W**). Warm-ups don't count towards personal bests or the total lifted.

**Not in this version yet**

- The home-screen widget, steps from the phone, the theme choice and spreadsheet export (0.10.0).

**Installing**

Download `fitness-0.9.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the last version and keeps your data.
