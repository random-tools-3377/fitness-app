Simple chilli con carne
Serves 4

Ingredients
1 tbsp olive oil
1 large onion, chopped
2 garlic cloves, crushed
500g lean beef mince
400g can chopped tomatoes
400g can kidney beans, drained
1 tsp chilli powder
1 tsp ground cumin
300ml beef stock
salt and pepper to taste

Method
Heat the oil in a large pan and soften the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices, then the mince, and brown it. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock, and simmer for 30 minutes.
